Logo
A spoon standing in a bowl, drawn as a checkmark — the can-make state as an object.
Construction
One silhouette, two readings. The bowl is a filled half-disc; the check is stroked wide in paprika so its overshoot past the rim reads as a spoon handle, then stroked narrow as a cut-out so the interior is transparent.
The overshoot is the whole idea — clipped to the rim it collapses back into a tick in a container. Olive stays out of the mark: in the app it means “you have this ingredient”, and inside the icon it just reads as a badge.
