Miso Butter Ramen
45 min
Comfort
Weeknight
Rich miso broth with browned butter, jammy eggs and charred scallions. The butter is the whole trick — brown it until it smells like toffee before the miso goes in.
Ingredients
6 of 8 in your kitchen
Ramen noodles
2 nests
Unsalted butter
3 tbsp
White miso paste
2 tbsp
Eggs
2 large
Scallions
4 stalks
Dried shiitake
6
Garlic
3 cloves
Soy sauce
1 tbsp
Instructions
1
Steep the shiitake in 900 ml of just-boiled water for 20 minutes. Keep the soaking liquid — that is your stock.
2
Brown the butter in a heavy pot until the milk solids turn amber and it smells nutty, then add the sliced garlic for 30 seconds.
3
Whisk the miso into a ladle of the warm stock before it goes in the pot, then pour in the rest with the soy sauce. Hold it below a simmer.
4
Boil the eggs for exactly 6 minutes 30 seconds, then straight into ice water. Peel and halve.
5
Cook the noodles separately so the starch stays out of the broth. Bowl them up, ladle over the broth, top with egg and charred scallion.
